Product Catalog

Comprehensive range of precision Flanges (Plate & Forged) and Forged Pipe Fittings conforming to international standards.

Industrial Flanges

Manufactured to ASME B16.5, B16.47, EN 1092-1. Available in Carbon, Stainless, and Alloy Steel.

Weld Neck Flange (WN)

ForgedClass 150-2500

Has a long tapered hub that is butt welded to the pipe.

  • Pros: Highest strength, excellent fatigue resistance.
  • Uses: Refineries, power plants, high pressure.

Slip-On Flange (SO)

ForgedCost Effective

Pipe passes through the flange; welded inside and outside.

  • Pros: Easy alignment, economical fabrication.
  • Uses: Water and low-pressure services.

Blind Flange (BL)

ForgedSealing

Solid flange used to seal pipeline ends or pressure vessels.

  • Pros: Easy maintenance access.
  • Uses: Pressure testing, future expansion.

Socket Weld Flange (SW)

ForgedSmall Bore

Pipe fits into a socket before fillet welding.

  • Uses: Small bore, high-pressure piping systems.

Threaded Flange (TH)

ForgedNo Welding

Internally threaded flange requiring no welding.

  • Uses: Hazardous areas where welding is undesirable.

Orifice Flange

ForgedFlow Measurement

Specialty flange with tapped holes for orifice meter flow measurement.

  • Uses: Flow rate measurement in pipelines.

Spectacle Blind Flange

ForgedLine Isolation

Figure-8 shaped plate for positive isolation of a pipeline section.

  • Uses: Safety isolation, maintenance shutdowns.

Long Weld Neck

PlateSealing

Plate-manufactured blind flange for sealing line ends.

  • Uses: Low to medium pressure sealing applications.

Lap Joint Flange

ForgedRotatable

Used with a Stub End; the flange rotates freely for easy bolt alignment.

  • Uses: Frequent dismantling, alloy piping systems.

Plate Slip-On Flange

PlateCost Effective

Plate-manufactured slip-on flange, economical for lower pressure duties.

  • Uses: Water, HVAC, low-pressure lines.

Pipe Fittings

Manufactured to ASME B16.11 under high pressure. Available in Socket Weld (SW) and Threaded (NPT/BSP) ends for small bore piping (Class 2000 - 9000).

90° Elbow (Socket & Threaded)

Forged

Changes pipe direction by 90°.

  • Uses: Steam, Hydraulics, Oil & Gas.

90° Long Radius Elbow

Buttweld

Bend radius 1.5x pipe diameter, minimizing flow resistance.

  • Uses: High-flow process piping.

Branch Outlet Fittings

Forged

Provides a strong, integrally reinforced branch connection welded directly onto a run pipe.

  • Uses: High-pressure branch connections, process piping.

Swage Nipple

ButtweldForged

Connects two pipes of different diameters; available in concentric and eccentric styles.

  • Uses: Pipe size transitions in process and utility lines.

90° Short Radius Elbow

Buttweld

Compact bend radius equal to pipe diameter.

  • Uses: Space-constrained piping layouts.

45° Elbow (Socket & Threaded)

Forged

Changes flow direction by 45° while minimizing pressure loss.

  • Uses: Process & utility lines.

45° Long Radius Elbow

Buttweld

Long bend radius for smooth directional change.

  • Uses: High-flow process piping.

45° Short Radius Elbow

Buttweld

Compact bend radius for tighter layouts.

  • Uses: Space-constrained piping.

Equal Tee

Buttweld

Creates a 90° branch connection with equal outlet diameters.

Unequal (Reducing) Tee

Forged

Branch connection with a reduced-size outlet.

Butt Weld Tee

Forged

Buttweld-end tee for large bore piping systems.

Cross

Forged

Provides four-way pipe connections.

  • Uses: Instrumentation and utility piping.

Coupling

Forged

Joins two pipes together in a straight line.

Union

Forged

Allows pipe disassembly without cutting.

  • Pros: Easy maintenance, reusable.

Cap

Buttweld

Closes the open end of a pipe.

Cap

Forged

Buttweld/threaded cap for terminating larger pipe runs.

Plug

Forged

Closes a female threaded opening.

Bushing & Boss

Forged

Bushing reduces threaded size. Boss provides a threaded outlet on tanks.

180° Bend (Return Bend)

Buttweld

Reverses flow direction by 180°.

  • Uses: Heat exchangers, coil piping.

Concentric Reducer

Buttweld

Joins two pipes of different diameters along a common centerline.

Eccentric Reducer

Buttweld

Joins two pipes of different diameters with one flat edge, keeping top or bottom level.

Stub End

Short / Long Pattern

Used with a Lap Joint Flange for rotatable pipe connections.
