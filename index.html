Product Catalog

Comprehensive range of precision fittings and flanges conforming to ASME B16.9, B16.11, and B16.5 standards.

45° & 90° Elbows

Seamless Welded ASME B16.9

Used to change the direction of flow. Available in Long Radius (LR) and Short Radius (SR).

Equal & Reducing Tees

Carbon Steel Stainless

Distribute or combine fluid flow. Precision machined ends for perfect weldability.

Concentric & Eccentric Reducers

Alloy Steel Custom

Connect pipes of different diameters. Eccentric used to maintain bottom of pipe elevation.

Weld Neck Flanges

ASME B16.5 150# - 2500#

Ideal for high-pressure and extreme temperature applications. Provides excellent stress distribution.

Slip-On Flanges

RF / FF ASTM A105

Slips over the pipe and welded inside and out. Cost-effective and easy to align during fabrication.

Blind Flanges

High Pressure RTJ

Used to seal the end of a piping system or pressure vessel opening. Handles extreme high stress.

Quality Assurance & Testing

Zero defect tolerance. Every component undergoes rigorous inspection to ensure absolute reliability.

1. Raw Material Inspection

PMI (Positive Material Identification) and chemical analysis of all incoming billets and plates.

2. In-Process Checks

Stringent dimensional inspection during forging and CNC machining stages to guarantee precise tolerances.

3. Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Ultrasonic Testing (UT), Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI), and Radiography to detect internal flaws.

4. Destructive Testing

Tensile strength, impact testing, and hardness testing conducted on sample batches.

5. Final Certification

Issue of EN10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificates and distinct marking for 100% material traceability.
